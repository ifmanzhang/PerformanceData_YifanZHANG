# Audio Stem Sources

All current stems are CC0 assets from OpenGameArt, downloaded for the local MVP so the audio layer can work offline.

| Local file | Role | Source | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `calm_pad_loop.ogg` | calm pad / bright opening layer | [Heavenly Loop](https://opengameart.org/content/heavenly-loop) | isaiah658 | CC0 |
| `contemplation_bed.mp3` | low-focus meditation bed | [Contemplation](https://opengameart.org/content/contemplation-0) | Joth | CC0 |
| `air_bells_loop.ogg` | airy bell / spacious texture | [Cathedral in the forest (ambient loop)](https://opengameart.org/content/cathedral-in-the-forest-ambient-loop) | congusbongus | CC0 |
| `pressure_cavern_loop.ogg` | dark pressure layer | [Dark Cavern Ambient](https://opengameart.org/content/dark-cavern-ambient) | Paul Wortmann | CC0 |

The app crossfades these stems according to `pressure` in `mvp/src/audio/audioEngine.js`. Voice cue text and mix parameters are still saved in exported JSON logs for later video/audio reconstruction.
